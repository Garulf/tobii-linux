usage: tobii-linux demo [-h] [--host HOST] [--port PORT]
                        [--display TLX TLY TLZ TRX TRY TRZ BLX BLY BLZ]
                        [--models MODELS] [--channel CHANNEL] [--index INDEX]
                        [--image-rate IMAGE_RATE] [--gaze-source {device}]
                        [--no-headpose] [--replay-frames LOG]
                        [--replay-frames-dir DIR] [--replay-gaze LOG]

Live browser view of the tracker: camera, head pose, gaze and status.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --host HOST           Address to listen on (default 127.0.0.1)
  --port PORT           HTTP port (default 8765)
  --display TLX TLY TLZ TRX TRY TRZ BLX BLY BLZ
                        Display area corners in millimetres: top-left, top-
                        right, bottom-left
  --models MODELS
  --channel CHANNEL     USB channel carrying TTP
  --index INDEX         Device index from probe
  --image-rate IMAGE_RATE
                        Camera images sent to the browser per second (default
                        15)
  --gaze-source {device}
  --no-headpose         Skip face tracking and head pose

replay:
  Play recorded data instead of opening the tracker

  --replay-frames LOG   JSON-lines log of tools/linux_session_probe.py --until
                        frames
  --replay-frames-dir DIR
                        Directory holding that log's frames (default: frames/
                        next to the log)
  --replay-gaze LOG     JSON-lines log of tools/linux_stream_capture.py with
                        gaze messages
